## SAR Summary (Tryptanthrin)
- **C-8 (D-ring)** is the primary potency driver: -NO₂ or -S-(2-pyridyl) → 5–50× improvement
- **A-ring aza substitution** (pyridine) enhances IDO/TDO binding; retains cytotoxicity
- **Multi-target** profile: EGFR, PI3K/AKT/mTOR, TGF-β, IDO inhibition confirmed across derivatives
- Carbonyl groups at C-6 and C-12 must be kept intact (H-bond anchors)

## R-group rules (passed to Nakshathra for analog generation)
- Position 8 (primary): -NO₂, -F, -Cl, -Br, -OCH₃, -CF₃, -S-(2-pyridyl), -NH₂
- Position 2/3 (A-ring): -F, -Cl, -OCH₃, -CH₃, -CF₃, or aza-substitution
- Position 9 (D-ring): small lipophilic (-F, -Cl, -CH₃, -OCH₃)
